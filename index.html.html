The Naga

Born from ancient myth. Built for modern warriors.

In Thai and Southeast Asian mythology, the Naga is a divine serpent of immense power — guardian of sacred waters, protector of jungle and mountain, bestower of strength to those worthy enough to enter its realm.

"The Naga doesn't live in cities. It lives deep in the jungle, coiled in the rivers, watching from the mountain peaks where the mist never fully clears."

That is where we built this camp. Deep in the highlands of northern Thailand — where the jungle meets the mountain and the ancient world is still very much alive.

⚔

For the competitor

World class Muay Thai training. Competition prep. Two sessions daily. Credentialed coaches who carry the ancient knowledge of the art.

🧠

For the warrior within

Some battles are fought internally. Naga Royals is a sanctuary for those using movement to battle mental health, overcome addiction, or reclaim who they are.

🌿

For the seeker

Weight loss. Life reset. Transformation. The jungle and the mountain don't care where you started. They only care that you showed up.

👑

For the family

Naga Royals is not a gym. It's a family. The bonds forged in this jungle — between fighters, coaches and community — last a lifetime.

The community
"When you train at Naga Royals, you don't just build yourself. You build a community. Every booking funds full-time employment for local families in Baan Huai Hoi."

The cook who feeds you from the jungle. The team who maintains the mountain. The children who train with you every Thursday. The Naga protects this land — and now so do you.

"Train here. Employ a family. Change a mountain."

Every Thursday the camp opens its doors. Local children and community members train alongside international fighters free of charge. In the evening — Muay Thai watch party. Jungle fire. One family.
